### 1. ACCA JHC Business Case Competition (Apr 2026) | Team captain | Advanced to South China regional round

Advised a consumer-electronics contract manufacturer (benchmarked against Foxconn; RMB 230bn revenue, 72% dependent on one North American client) on an AI-driven carbon-neutrality strategy. The client demanded Scope 3 carbon data traceability rise from 62% to 95% by end-2026 — or lose 15% of orders (RMB 24.84bn).

- **Campus round**: Framed the problem and quantified order-flow risk — a 10% order loss drives a 98.8% profit decline
- **Financial analysis**: Built detailed cost and cash-flow models for two delivery models — milestone (40%-30%-30%) and EMC — and proposed risk mitigation; flagged EMC's first-year negative net cash flow of RMB 3.04m
- **Business model design**: Differentiated analysis of the market and the company's capabilities; designed the business model and revenue projection; proposed an SaaS platform plus consulting service to plug the cash-flow gap
- **Subscription market research**: Studied 6 subscription-style services on the market, set a defensible pricing scheme, and worked backwards to define milestone targets
- **Five-layer cross-validation system**: document verification → consistency check → external verification → site inspection → uncertainty assessment (per GHG Protocol / ISO 14064)
- **Data audit working paper**: an 8-column structure (assumption → value → source → link → verification status → evidence screenshot → authority → summary), validating a single assumption against 7 independent data sources

→ Full case: [case-studies / ACCA-JHC](https://github.com/yyj710/case-studies/blob/main/02-ACCA-JHC/CASE.md)
